🔄 The 5-Step Backstage Pathway

How funds travel securely from promoter escrow to payee accounts:

  1. Digital Agreement & Escrow Setup: Promoter and talent manager lock terms, milestone dates, and fee splits.
  2. Pre-Show Escrow Funding: Total gross fee is deposited into an insured escrow vault 48 hours before doors open.
  3. Milestone Sign-Off: Production and tour liaisons digitally sign off on the completed set.
  4. Instant Split Disbursement: Clearing engine parses funds via InstaPay/PESONet simultaneously (80% artist, 15% agency, 5% crew).
  5. Official Receipt Emission: An immutable digital official receipt is generated for instant tax reconciliation.
